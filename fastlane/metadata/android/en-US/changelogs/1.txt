First release. Alpha software — expect rough edges.

• Pure-Kotlin SCAD engine: lexer, parser, evaluator, CSG and 2D geometry, with
  49 unit tests. No JNI, no native code.
• Orbit/zoom/pan viewport with distance-based auto-framing.
• Parameter sliders generated from model variables, re-evaluating live.
• Syntax-highlighted editor, console panel.
• Opens .scad via file manager or share sheet. Exports STL.
• Light and dark Material 3.

Gaps: STL only, flat shading, no write-back, slow on large models. Tested on
x86_64; arm64 not yet verified on hardware.
