First release. Alpha software — expect rough edges.

• Pure-Kotlin SCAD engine: lexer, parser, evaluator, CSG, 2D geometry. 49 unit
  tests. No JNI, no native code.
• Orbit/zoom/pan viewport with auto-framing.
• Parameter sliders from model variables, re-evaluating live.
• Highlighted editor, console, .scad file open, STL export.
• Light and dark Material 3.

Gaps: STL only, flat shading, no write-back, slow on large models. Tested on
x86_64; arm64 not yet verified on hardware.
